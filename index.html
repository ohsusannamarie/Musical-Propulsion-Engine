What the engine is actually measuring

This is not a “best musicals” list. It is a map of how strongly a musical experience is likely to create physical, vocal, or emotional participation for you.

Propulsion ≠ upbeat

A devastating song can score extremely high if it builds, releases, hooks you, and makes you need to participate. Sad is allowed. Boring is not.

Beltability ≠ familiarity

You do not need to know a lyric. The question is whether your nervous system starts singing anyway.

Movement ≠ choreography

The cast can stand still. What matters is whether you want to move, sway, conduct, dance, gesture, or otherwise join the event.

Musical DNA

Style describes what it sounds like: pop, glam rock, hip-hop, soul, synth, Broadway, gospel, Latin, jukebox, and more.

Vibe

Vibe describes what it feels like: euphoric, yearning, feral, cathartic, devastating, comforting, weird, triumphant, communal.

Connections have reasons

A recommendation should tell you the bridge: Lin-Manuel lyrical density, theatrical maximalism, identity anthem, emotional escalation, rock-opera structure, etc.
